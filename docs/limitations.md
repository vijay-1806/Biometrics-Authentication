# System Limitations & Scope

## Empirical Evaluation Scope
- **Population**: Tested on 5 independent candidate profiles (N = 100 evaluation attempts).
- **Concurrency**: Validated up to 10 concurrent active candidates submitting telemetry every 5 seconds.
- **Hardware/Device Scope**: Single-machine local sandbox environment (Intel/Linux).

## Future Research Recommendations
- Expand candidate pool across diverse physical keyboard layouts (laptop vs mechanical).
- Conduct multi-week longitudinal study to model long-term behavioral drift.
